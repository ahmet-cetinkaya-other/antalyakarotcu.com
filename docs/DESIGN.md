## 1. System Overview

### 1.1 Purpose

This design document defines the operational framework, technical methodologies, and resource architecture for **Antalya Karotcu**. It translates the business requirements for high-precision concrete services into actionable field strategies, ensuring standardization across nationwide operations.

### 1.2 Scope

The design encompasses the full lifecycle of the following service pillars:

- **Core Services:** Concrete drilling (Karot), Cutting (Beton Kesme), and Demolition (Duvar Kırma).
- **Structural Services:** Chemical Anchorage (Kimyasal Ankraj) and Rebar Planting (Filiz Ekimi).
- **Infrastructure Services:** Asphalt Cutting and Excavation (Hafriyat).

## 2. Operational Architecture

### 2.1 Service Delivery Workflow

The operational logic follows a sequential "Assess-Plan-Execute-Verify" model to ensure safety and precision.

#### **Phase 1: Project Intake & Assessment**

- **Input:** Client inquiry via Phone/WhatsApp (+90 532 609 68 02).
- **Triage:** Categorization of needs (e.g., Structural Analysis vs. Renovation).
- **Output:** Preliminary technical scope and quotation.

#### **Phase 2: Site Mobilization**

- **Logistics:** Deployment of "Mobile Response Teams" to the site location.
- **Safety Audit:** Pre-work site survey to identify load-bearing structures and hidden utilities.

#### **Phase 3: Execution (Technical Core)**

- **Setup:** Installation of shoring (support) and dust suppression systems.
- **Operation:** Execution using specific technical methods (detailed in Section 3).
- **Clean-up:** Removal of concrete cores/debris and slurry management.

#### **Phase 4: Quality Assurance & Handover**

- **Verification:** Measurement of cut precision (Target: ±2mm) and structural integrity check.
- **Sign-off:** Client approval and handover.

## 3. Technical Methodologies & Specifications

### 3.1 Concrete Core Drilling (Karot)

- **Method:** Wet Diamond Drilling.
- **Equipment:** Stand-mounted electric or hydraulic core drills.
- **Specifications:**
- **Diameter:** 10mm – 500mm+.
- **Depth:** Variable (Project dependent).
- **Standard:** Samples extracted must comply with **TS EN 12504** for valid lab testing.

- **Critical Control:** Vibration-free operation to prevent micro-cracks in the sample.

### 3.2 Concrete Cutting (Beton Kesme)

The system selects the cutting method based on structural thickness and accessibility:

| Method                     | Application Criteria                | Technical Advantage                        |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| **Hydraulic Rail Cutting** | Walls/Floors needing straight cuts. | High precision, track-mounted stability.   |
| **Wire Rope (Halatlı)**    | Thick/Reinforced concrete masses.   | Unlimited depth, cuts through heavy rebar. |
| **Floor Sawing**           | Horizontal slabs (Roads/Floors).    | Fast execution, uniform depth control.     |

### 3.3 Structural Strengthening

- **Chemical Anchorage (Kimyasal Ankraj):**
- **Process:** Drill → **Clean (Critical)** → Inject Epoxy → Insert Stud.
- **Material:** Epoxy, Polyester, or Vinyl ester resins.

- **Rebar Planting (Filiz Ekimi):**
- **Use Case:** Connecting new concrete to old structures or repairing damaged columns.
- **Requirement:** Adherence to **TS 500** standards for reinforced concrete.

### 3.4 Excavation & Infrastructure

- **Asphalt Cutting:** Use of diamond-tipped walk-behind saws for clean infrastructure cuts (up to 300mm depth).
- **Excavation:** Systematic removal using heavy machinery (Excavators/Backhoes) with strict slope stability (iksa) protocols.

## 4. Resource & Equipment Strategy

### 4.1 Equipment Fleet

To support the "Nationwide Coverage" and "Technology Adoption" goals, the fleet is structured into:

- **Precision Tools:** Diamond core drills, Hilti-type breakers.
- **Heavy Machinery:** Hydraulic wire saws, rail saws, excavators.
- **Support Units:** \* Vacuum systems for dust control (Wet Method).
- Generators for remote site power.
- Safety barriers and scaffolding.

### 4.2 Personnel Structure

- **Project Managers:** Oversee client coordination and resource allocation.
- **Lead Technicians:** Certified experts in hydraulic and diamond equipment operation.
- **Safety Officers:** Enforce PPE usage and site safety protocols.

---

## 5. Quality & Safety Assurance (HSE)

### 5.1 Safety Protocols

- **Personal Protective Equipment (PPE):** Mandatory Helmets, High-Vis Vests, Steel-toe Boots, Gloves, and Ear Protection.
- **Risk Mitigation:**
- **Structural:** Pre-demolition assessment to prevent collapse.
- **Health:** Water suppression systems to eliminate silica dust exposure.

### 5.2 Quality Metrics (KPIs)

- **Precision:** Dimensional tolerance within ±2mm for cuts.
- **Efficiency:** On-time completion rate ≥ 95%.
- **Customer:** CSAT Score ≥ 4.5/5.
