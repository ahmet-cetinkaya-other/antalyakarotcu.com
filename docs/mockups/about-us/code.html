Our History

Building a
Legacy

From a single core drill unit in Antalya to a nationwide fleet of heavy industrial machinery. Our journey is paved with concrete solutions.

1995
The Beginning

Founded in Antalya

Antalya Karotcu was established to meet the growing demand for precise concrete modification in the region's booming tourism construction sector. Starting with just two operators, we set a new standard for cleanliness and accuracy.

2008
Expansion

Heavy Machinery Fleet

We acquired our first remote-controlled demolition robots and heavy-duty wire saws, allowing us to take on massive infrastructure projects including bridges and dams across the Mediterranean region.

2015
Innovation

Technical Certification

Achieved ISO 9001:2015 certification and implemented industry-leading safety protocols. Our team grew to over 50 certified specialists, handling complex chemical anchoring and seismic retrofitting projects.

2023
Today

National Leadership

With over 5,000 successful projects, we are now the preferred partner for Turkey's largest construction firms. Our fleet is fully modernized with the latest diamond cutting technology from Europe.
