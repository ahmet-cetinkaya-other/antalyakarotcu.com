## 1. Executive Summary

### 1.1 Company Overview

**Antalya Karotcu** is a pioneering construction services company established in 1995, operating nationwide across Turkey. With over 28 years of experience, the company has established itself as a leader in concrete drilling, cutting, and structural strengthening services.

- **Nationwide Coverage:** Services available throughout Turkey
- **Modern Technology:** Diamond-tipped and hydraulic cutting methods

## 5. Product Features & Services

### 5.1 Karot (Core Drilling)

**Service Description:**
Taking cylindrical concrete samples from structures for quality testing, structural analysis, and earthquake resistance assessment.

**Key Features:**

- Diamond-tipped cylindrical drilling equipment
- Precise sample collection for laboratory testing
- Structural integrity assessment
- Earthquake resistance measurement
- Building lifespan evaluation
- Non-destructive to surrounding structure

**Use Cases:**

- Building quality certification
- Structural safety assessment
- Pre-purchase building inspection
- Regulatory compliance testing
- Earthquake resistance evaluation

**Technical Approach:**

- Core sampling from walls, columns, slabs
- Sizes: Custom diameters based on requirements
- Depth: Variable, project-dependent
- Testing: Compression testing, visual inspection, material analysis

### 5.2 Beton Delme (Concrete Drilling)

**Service Description:**
Precision drilling in concrete for openings, installations, and structural modifications.

**Key Features:**

- Diamond-tipped drilling bits
- Wet and dry cutting methods
- Various diameters (10mm to 500mm+)
- Dust-free operation (wet method)
- Precision positioning
- Vibration-free drilling

**Use Cases:**

- HVAC duct openings
- Electrical conduit routing
- Plumbing installations
- Cable and wire passages
- Anchor bolt installation
- Sensor and equipment mounting

**Technical Specifications:**

- Methods: Wet drilling (dust-free), dry drilling (quick)
- Equipment: Diamond core drills, rotary hammers
- Diameters: 10mm - 500mm+
- Depth: Up to 1m standard, deeper on request
- Surface compatibility: Concrete, reinforced concrete, granite, asphalt

### 5.3 Beton Kesme (Concrete Cutting)

**Service Description:**
Precision cutting of concrete structures for openings, modifications, and demolition.

**Cutting Methods:**

#### 5.3.1 Hydraulic Rail Cutting

- Wall-mounted rail systems for straight cuts
- Ideal for walls, floors, ceilings
- High precision and clean cuts
- Minimal vibration and noise

#### 5.3.2 Wire Rope Cutting (Halatlı Kesme)

- Diamond-beaded wire cutting
- Ideal for thick concrete, large structures
- Can cut reinforced concrete with rebar
- Suitable for complex shapes and curves

#### 5.3.3 Floor Sawing

- Walk-behind floor saws
- For horizontal cuts on slabs
- Precision depth control
- Dust control options

**Use Cases:**

- Door and window openings
- Wall and floor modifications
- Expansion joint creation
- Structural demolition
- Equipment installation access
- HVAC shaft creation

**Technical Specifications:**

- Cut depth: Up to 600mm (floor saw), unlimited (wire saw)
- Precision: ±2mm
- Power: Electric, hydraulic, or diesel options
- Dust control: Water suppression, vacuum systems

### 5.4 Asfalt Kesme (Asphalt Cutting)

**Service Description:**
Precision cutting and removal of asphalt layers for road work, utilities, and infrastructure projects.

**Key Features:**

- Specialized asphalt cutting machines
- Diamond or carbide-tipped blades
- Straight, clean cuts
- Minimal damage to surrounding area
- Quick execution

**Use Cases:**

- Road repairs and patching
- Utility line access
- Drainage installation
- Road widening
- Parking lot modifications
- Municipal roadworks

**Technical Specifications:**

- Cutting depth: Up to 300mm standard, deeper on request
- Width: Adjustable, typically 10-50mm
- Equipment: Walk-behind asphalt saws
- Dust control: Water suppression

### 5.5 Duvar Kırma (Wall Breaking)

**Service Description:**
Controlled demolition of walls and concrete structures.

**Methods:**

#### 5.5.1 Partial Wall Breaking

- Precision tools (chisels, hammers)
- For specific sections
- Minimal disturbance to surroundings

#### 5.5.2 Complete Wall Demolition

- Heavy equipment (sledgehammers, breakers)
- For full wall removal
- Efficient for large areas

#### 5.5.3 Hydraulic Breaking

- Hydraulic breakers
- For reinforced concrete
- Controlled, powerful demolition

**Use Cases:**

- Interior layout modifications
- Room expansion or removal
- Structural alterations
- Renovation projects
- Emergency wall removal

**Safety Considerations:**

- Structural assessment before demolition
- Load-bearing wall identification
- Proper shoring and support
- Dust and debris control
- Personal protective equipment (PPE)

### 5.6 Kimyasal Ankraj (Chemical Anchorage)

**Service Description:**
Structural strengthening using chemical adhesive anchors for reinforcement.

**Key Features:**

- High-strength epoxy or chemical adhesives
- Reinforcement bar installation in existing concrete
- Column and beam strengthening
- Structural load transfer

**Applications:**

- Steel column and beam connections
- Steel roof applications
- Machinery installation
- Flag pole mounting
- Exterior facade applications
- Building strengthening projects
- Building expansion projects
- Staircase reinforcement (forgotten rebars)
- Hidden rebar installation

**Technical Process:**

1. Drilling holes to required depth and diameter
2. Cleaning holes of dust and debris
3. Injecting chemical adhesive (epoxy/resin)
4. Inserting reinforcement bars
5. Curing time for adhesive to set
6. Load testing (if required)

**Materials:**

- Epoxy adhesives
- Polyester resins
- Vinyl ester resins
- Reinforcement bars (various diameters)

### 5.7 Filiz Ekimi (Rebar Planting)

**Service Description:**
Adding reinforcement to existing concrete structures to strengthen and repair damaged elements.

**Key Features:**

- Structural strengthening of existing concrete
- Repair of damaged or deteriorated structures
- Load capacity improvement
- Seismic retrofitting

**Use Cases:**

- Damaged column repair
- Beam strengthening
- Slab reinforcement
- Seismic retrofitting
- Building capacity upgrades
- Structural crack repair

**Technical Process:**

1. Surface preparation and cleaning
2. Drilling holes for reinforcement
3. Epoxy or chemical bonding application
4. Reinforcement bar insertion
5. Bonding and curing
6. Quality verification

**Benefits:**

- Increased structural capacity
- Extended building lifespan
- Improved earthquake resistance
- Cost-effective alternative to reconstruction
- Minimal disruption to building use

### 5.8 Hafriyat (Excavation)

**Service Description:**
Professional excavation and earthworks for construction and infrastructure projects.

**Key Features:**

- Professional excavation equipment
- Experienced operators
- Site safety protocols
- Environmental compliance
- Infrastructure protection

**Pre-Excavation Requirements:**

- Site survey and analysis
- Geotechnical investigation
- Soil testing and classification
- Infrastructure mapping (utilities, cables, pipes)
- Risk assessment

**Use Cases:**

- Building foundations
- Utility trenching
- Site preparation
- Landscaping
- Drainage systems
- Road construction
- Demolition debris removal

**Safety Protocols:**

- Slope stability and shoring (iksa)
- Hazard identification (explosives, chemicals, contaminants)
- Underground utility verification
- Site access control
- Personnel safety equipment

**Equipment:**

- Excavators
- Backhoes
- Dump trucks
- Compactors
- Graders
