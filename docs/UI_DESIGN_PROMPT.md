Context: Design a professional, high-fidelity responsive landing page for "Antalya Karotcu," a heavy construction services company established in 1995. The target audience is construction site managers, engineers, and government contractors who value safety and precision.

Aesthetic: "Engineered Solidity" — industrial, heavy, stable, yet precise.

Headings/Footer: Concrete Grey (#2C3E50) to imply stability.
Accents/Icons: Steel Blue (#34495E) for technical precision.
Background: Dust White (#F5F6FA) for cleanliness.
Typography: Use 'Barlow' or 'Teko' (condensed sans-serif) for headings to look like architectural blueprints. Use 'Inter' or 'Roboto' for body text.
