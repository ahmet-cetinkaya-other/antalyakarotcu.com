# Gallery Images

Add your images files (JPG, PNG, WEBP) directly into these category folders.

The folders correspond to the categories in the gallery:

- **beton-kesme**: Beton Kesme related images
- **karot-delme**: Karot Delme related images
- **yikim**: Yıkım related images
- **ankraj**: Ankraj related images
- **diger**: Other images

## How it works
- The **filename** becomes the title of the image (e.g., `akdeniz-universitesi-guclendirme.jpg` -> "Akdeniz Universitesi Guclendirme").
- The **folder name** determines the category.
- Just drop the files here, and they will automatically appear on the /galeri page.
